# Theme system — master plan (9 Nov 2026)

## Decision
3 master themes, one per plan. Same engine, same data, same booking engine.

| Plan | Master | Pages | Booking features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | NEST | Home (rooms inline) · Room detail · About+Location+Contact · Policies | Room + dates → guest → pay at property / bKash manual → confirm |
| Business | CITY | + Rooms list · Gallery · Facilities · Offers · Contact | + extras, coupon, 30% advance, manage booking |
| Ultra | GRAND | + Dining · Experiences · Events enquiry · My Trip | + dining pre-order / table request, upsell |
| Enterprise | GRAND + custom sections | | |

- Each master has **10 colour themes** (hotel chooses) and Hotel / Resort photo style.
- Upgrade → next master activates at once (content comes from HMS, nothing to re-type).
- Downgrade → lower master; extra pages hidden, data kept.
- A higher plan may choose a lower master (e.g. small property on Ultra wants NEST).
- Pages with no data (Dining, Experiences, Offers, Events) hide automatically.
