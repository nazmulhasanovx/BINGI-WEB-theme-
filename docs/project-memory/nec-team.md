# NEC TEAM (BinGi Team) internal app

- **Purpose:** an internal app to replace WhatsApp lead tracking. Lead management is priority #1; team management is #2.
- **V1 modules:** Dashboard, Leads, Follow-up, Visits, Tasks, Issues, Team, Activity, Notifications, Search, Roles, Profile.
- **Lead statuses:** New, Contacted, Interested, Follow-up, Visit Scheduled, Negotiation, Won, Lost.
- **V2:** iOS-style design with 10 prompts (NEC-P01–P10).
- **Known issues:**
  - The lead record has no room count or plan-interest field.
  - Opening a single lead displays incorrectly.
