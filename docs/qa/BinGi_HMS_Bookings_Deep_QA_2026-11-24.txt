  WHERE     Finance > Overview / Ledger

            But every cash and manual-bKash payment taken at the

              BinGi Commission    -3.00

            holds in its own drawer.

            minus deductions (6,10,829.14) - 319.70 extra.
  EXPECTED  Only money that reached BinGi (online gateway, BinGi
            Marketplace) goes to Finance payable / payout.
            Cash and the hotel's own bKash stay in Cashbox / hotel
            accounts. No commission on walk-in / phone / cash.
            (22 Nov decision "Payments via BinGi".)
  FIX       BE: post to Finance only when payment channel = BinGi
            gateway / marketplace. Fix payable arithmetic.

  Stay & room: "Actual Arrival - Time not recorded" right after

  #13: header "Balance 0 - Fee 0 - refund due 10,000";
  Payments tab "Balance Due BDT 54,000"; refund panel
  "Available credit 0".

[DEEP-P1-06] Refund cannot be finished by the hotel
  Refund needs "Execute the approved refund in the existing Super
  Admin refund workflow" (developer text shown to staff).
  Refund methods only Cash Return / Bank Transfer - no bKash back,
  though the guest paid by bKash.
  The same admin who requests the cancel also approves it.

[DEEP-P1-07] No-show is saved as "Cancelled", fee not tracked

  "Guest requested cancellation" (I wrote "did not arrive").

  list, Payments, Reports. No-show allowed at 13:30 on arrival day.
  EXPECTED  Status "No-show", allowed after the arrival day / night
  audit, fee counted as receivable.

[DEEP-P1-08] Outstanding differs between Reports and Bookings

  "Departures 0" while 3 guests checked out today and 5 stays are
  past checkout. Prototype: Arrivals = still to check in,
  Departures = due out today (incl. overdue).

[DEEP-P1-10] Early check-out keeps the full stay
  #5 (24-26 Nov) checked out on the 24th: no prompt, still shows
  "2 nights", full charge, no shorten / refund option.
  (Room 105 was correctly released for 25-26.)

[DEEP-P1-11] Reports count future stays as today's sales
  "Daily sales today 13,51,576" = all bookings made today, incl.
