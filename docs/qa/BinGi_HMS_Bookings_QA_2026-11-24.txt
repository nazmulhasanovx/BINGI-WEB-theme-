================================================================

[BKG-P0-01] Occupied rooms can be booked and checked in again
  WHERE     New booking -> Rooms (tonight 24 -> 25 Nov)
  HAPPENS   Rooms with guests still IN HOUSE (checkout overdue):
              104 (Saimon Hasan, WBK-2026-000077)
              102 (NaZmul, WBK-2026-000043)
              206 (Yeapes, WBK-2026-000011)
              208 (Saimon Hasan, WBK-2026-000064)
              209 (test, WBK-2026-000010)
            All show as free ("5 of 5 free", "4 of 4 free").
            I booked 104 -> saved (WBK-2026-000079).
            Check-in review said "Room is clean and ready - Ready".
            Check-in confirmed. Room 104 now has TWO in-house guests.
  IMPACT    Two guests sent to the same room. Front desk cannot
            trust availability.
  EXPECTED  A room is not free while an in-house stay is in it,
            even after its planned checkout date (overdue).
            Check-in is blocked if the room has another in-house
            stay.
  FIX       BE: availability = no overlapping stay with status
            confirmed / in-house, where an in-house stay's end is
            max(planned checkout, today+1) until checked out.
            BE: block check-in when room has an in-house stay.

[BKG-P0-02] "Email (optional)" but booking fails without it
  WHERE     New booking -> Guest (no email) -> Confirm booking
  HAPPENS   Error: "The guest info.email field is required when
            guest info.booking mode is detailed."
            The Guest step labels Email as optional.
  IMPACT    Walk-in guests without email cannot be booked.
            Message is developer text.
  EXPECTED  Email optional for walk-in / phone bookings (phone is
            the key in BD). If needed, ask on the Guest step.
  FIX       BE: drop "required_if detailed" for email, or FE: mark
            it required. Map all API errors to plain sentences.

[BKG-P0-03] Room board does not match bookings
  WHERE     Bookings -> Room board (Room Overview)
  HAPPENS   Occupied = 3 (101, 102, 104).
            But in-house bookings are in 102, 104, 206, 208, 209.
            101 shows "Currently occupied" with no in-house booking.
            206, 208, 209 show "Clean / Available".
            202 is "Out of Order" on the board but New booking lists
            202 as free.
  IMPACT    Housekeeping and front desk see a different hotel from
            the booking list. Rooms may be sold twice or sold while
            blocked.
  EXPECTED  Room occupancy comes from in-house stays; out-of-order
            rooms are never offered in New booking.
  FIX       BE: one source of truth for room occupancy; availability
            query excludes out-of-order / blocked rooms.

 P2 - POLISH
================================================================
  1. List "Room / unit" shows "Twin" for every booking. Show the room
     category (Deluxe, Premium Room ...).
  2. Booking page "Room Type: Room" instead of "Deluxe".
     Check-out table also says "Room".
  3. Two booking number series: WBK-... (walk-in) and OBK-... (online).
     Fine if intended; show the source next to it.
  4. Source names: "BinGi Marketplace" vs "Bingi Mobile App" -
     use "BinGi" everywhere.
  5. Money format mixes "BDT 266" and "৳266" on the same screens,
     and decimals like 2,969.14.
  6. "1 Nights", "CONFIRMED · Unpaid" (capitals) in check-in review.
  7. Two categories both named "Suite" (৳41,000 and ৳60,000).
     Ask for unique names.
  8. Meal added to booking for "24 Nov 08:00" (already past) - accepted.
     Default to the next meal time; warn on past times.
  9. Direct discount allows up to the full total (৳266 of ৳266).
     Apply the cashier discount limit, needs a reason (it does).
 10. Room board text is long and technical ("This is a review
     checklist, not check-in approval. Category/type/location
     eligibility ..."). Shorten for staff.
 11. Room board breadcrumb says "Table", URL /bookings/table.
 12. Room board: every room "Floor not set".
 13. Collect payment shows "bKash" twice (same as Meals).
 14. Booking meals wait as "Counter Pending" (approval works here),
     but QR meal orders skip approval (see MEAL-P0-02) - align.
 15. Test data: unit type "Tow bad room", ৳16 room prices,
     lorem dish names. Clean before demos.
 16. Slow: confirm / check-in / payment take 5-7 s.

 WORKS - KEEP
================================================================
  - Counters, tabs (All, Arrivals, In House, Departures, Payment
    Due), search, status and source filters, "Needs attention".
  - "Checkout overdue" and "Needs review" tags in the list.
  - New booking 4 steps match prototype: Rooms & rates -> Guest ->
    Meals & services -> Payment & review, with live summary.
  - Guest step: saved guest search, ID type, ID photos, stay
    register (booked by / who pays), other guests, preferences.
  - Booking page tabs: Overview, Stay & room, Guests, Bill & folio,
    Payments, Meals & services, Documents, Messages, Activity.
    Quick links: confirmation PDF, contract, letter, invoice.
  - Check-in review checklist (room ready, guest, ID, advance,
    balance, actual time) with confirmation tick.
  - Partial payment: amount, received, change, "I have received
    the cash"; Paid / Balance update correctly; "Partly paid".
  - Check-out review blocks completion while balance is due or a
    meal / service order is pending, and explains why.
  - Footer note: "Booking status, payment and room cleaning are kept
    separate" - matches our rule.
