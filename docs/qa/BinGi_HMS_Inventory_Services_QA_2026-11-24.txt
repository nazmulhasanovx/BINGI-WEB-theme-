---------------- P1 ----------------

[INV-P1-01] Receiving at a new price re-prices ALL old stock
  WHERE     Receive / PO receive
  HAPPENS   Item cost is replaced by the last price, for all stores.
            Example:
              Main Store had 50 kg rice @ ৳80 = ৳4,000
              Received 50 kg @ ৳90 into QA Kitchen
              -> Main Store's old 50 kg became ৳90 too (৳4,400 -> ৳4,900)
              Then PO receipt 25 kg @ ৳95 -> all 115 kg now @ ৳95
            Form text even says: "A unit cost entered here replaces
            the item's current unit cost."
  IMPACT    Stock value, food cost and cost of goods are wrong
            whenever prices change. Accounts get wrong numbers.
  EXPECTED  Weighted average cost (prototype):
            (old qty x old cost + new qty x new cost) / total qty
            Example above: (50x80 + 50x90) / 100 = ৳85
  FIX       BE: weighted average on every receipt. Movement keeps
            its own unit cost for history.

[INV-P1-02] Issue / Wastage / Adjust forms can change cost and supplier
  WHERE     Issue, Wastage, Physical count adjustment drawers
  HAPPENS   These forms show Supplier + Unit cost fields with
            "replaces the item's current unit cost".
            Supplier is saved on the movement: History shows
            "QA Fresh Supply" on Issued and Wastage lines.
  EXPECTED  Supplier + cost only on Receive. Issue/wastage use the
            current (average) cost automatically.
  FIX       FE: hide fields. BE: ignore cost/supplier for out moves.

[INV-P1-03] Wastage saved without a reason
  WHERE     Wastage
  HAPPENS   Saved with empty note. History shows "-".
  EXPECTED  Reason required for Wastage and Adjustment (prototype).
            Quick picks Damaged / Expired / Spilled already exist.
  FIX       FE + BE validation.

[INV-P1-04] Drawer title and close (X) hidden under the top bar
  WHERE     Stock count drawer, New Purchase Order drawer,
            Purchase order details, Add Recipe drawer
  HAPPENS   Drawer starts at top 0; the app header (63 px) covers
            its title (e.g. "CNT-0001 - QA Kitchen") and X button.
  EXPECTED  Same as Add Item / Transfer drawers: title + X visible.
  FIX       FE: z-index / top offset of these drawers.

[INV-P1-05] Buying unit (sack, carton...) not used anywhere
  WHERE     Purchase order, Receive
  HAPPENS   Item says "1 sack = 25 kg. Orders round up to whole
            sacks." But PO accepted 30 kg (= "1.2 sack").
            PO and Receive only allow kg, never sacks.
  EXPECTED  PO and Receive can enter in sacks; PO rounds up to
            whole buying units; stock is stored in kg.

[INV-P1-06] Opening stock always goes to Main Store
  WHERE     Add Item
  HAPPENS   No store choice for opening stock.
  EXPECTED  "Opening stock is in: [store]" (prototype) when
            opening stock > 0.

[INV-P1-07] Recipes are not linked to Restaurant & Bar (Meals)
  WHERE     Recipes & sales
  HAPPENS   Dish name is free text. Sales are typed by hand.
  EXPECTED  Recipe links to a Meals menu item. Every closed
            restaurant order takes ingredients out of stock
            automatically (prototype note). Manual sales stay as
            a backup.
  FIX       BE: integration Meals order -> recipe -> stock out.

---------------- P2 ----------------
  1. Units: only piece, kg, litre, packet, box.
     Add bottle, roll, set (prototype), and gram / ml.
  2. Item with no SKU shows the unit ("kg") where SKU should be.
     Auto-make a SKU like prototype (e.g. RICE-001).
  3. Supplier phone accepts "123". Validate BD mobile (11 digits).
  4. Category / supplier that has items still shows a Delete icon.
     Disable it and say why.
  5. Receive form starts with the first item already picked (DENTAL).
     Start with "Choose item" to avoid receiving the wrong item.
  6. Number inputs with unit (kg) show a broken border / the focus
     ring covers the unit.
  7. Add Item: hint "Enter 0 if this item has no stock yet" sits
     under Unit Cost. Move it to Opening Stock.
  8. Record sales: no warning before save when stock is not enough;
     server error comes after ~5 s. Check in the form. Add sale date.
  9. PO partial receipt changed the PO total from ৳2,700 to ৳2,850
     (all lines re-priced). Keep ordered value; show received value
     separately. "5 still due across lines" has no unit (5 kg).
 10. PO has no Print / PDF / Send to supplier.
 11. Stock count posts at once: no review screen, no reason for a
     difference, system qty shown while counting (prefer blind count).
 12. History "Balance" is the property total, not the store's balance.
 13. Slow: tab switch and saves often take 3-6 s.
 14. Inventory drawers use a dark backdrop; Services drawers use a
     light one. Use one drawer style (design: no dark overlays).
 15. Inventory "Suppliers" and Services "Vendors" are two separate
     lists. Consider one shared vendor list.
 16. Number formats differ: PO-0001, CNT-0001, SALE-0001, GRN-0001
     vs Services "OSO-GIBQD2W45S". Use one readable pattern.
 17. Money shown as "BDT" in Inventory/Services, "Tk." in Cashbox.
     Use one (৳ or BDT) everywhere.

---------------- WORKS - KEEP ----------------
  - Tabs and layout match the prototype: Stock, Purchase orders,
    Stock count, Recipes & sales, History, Reports, Setup.
  - Header actions: Add Item, Transfer, Issue, Receive.
  - Stores (Main + own stores), categories, suppliers.
  - Transfer blocks more than the store has ("Enter 20 kg or less").
  - PO receive blocks more than ordered ("Up to 30 kg").
  - Recipe sales blocked when stock is short (clear message).
  - Partial PO receipt -> "Partly received", stays open.
  - History cannot be edited or deleted; Asia/Dhaka time.
  - Reports: stock value, low/out of stock, movements, cost of goods
    used by category, stock value by store. All numbers correct.
  - Food cost and food cost % on recipes are correct.

---------------- P1 ----------------

[SRV-P1-01] New scheduled order goes straight to "Active"
  WHERE     New Service Order -> Schedule (tomorrow), nobody assigned
  HAPPENS   Board shows it in "Active - open work to deliver".
            Edit Order shows status = Active.
  EXPECTED  Future + nobody assigned = "Scheduled".
            Assigned, not started = "Assigned".
            Started = "Active".
  FIX       BE: default status from requested time + assignee.

[SRV-P1-02] Order details has only "Mark done"
  WHERE     Click an order card -> Order Details
  HAPPENS   Buttons: Close, Edit Order, Mark done.
            No Assign, no Start. Assign only inside Edit Order.
  EXPECTED  Assign (pick staff) and Start buttons in details, same
            steps as the board: Assign -> Start -> Done.

[SRV-P1-03] Save errors are hidden at the bottom of the form
  WHERE     New Service Order -> Save
  HAPPENS   "Order not confirmed - Open today's cashbox ..." and
            "QA Car 1 is already booked 10:00-11:00 ..." appear
            below the total, out of view. Looks like Save did nothing.
  EXPECTED  Error shown at the top of the visible area (or toast),
            and the form scrolls to it.

[SRV-P1-04] Cash sale only: no "pay later", only cash
  WHERE     New Service Order -> Cash sale
  HAPPENS   A walk-in order scheduled for tomorrow must be
            "Cash sale - paid now" and needs today's cashbox open.
            No bKash / Nagad / card choice.
  EXPECTED  Walk-in: Pay now (cash / bKash / Nagad / card) or
            Pay when done. Booking guests: Charge to booking.
            Info box should say up front if the cashbox is closed.

[SRV-P1-05] No "Charged per" and no child price
  WHERE     Add Service
  HAPPENS   Only one "Unit price". Quantity on the order has no
            meaning (person? trip? hour?).
  EXPECTED  Charged: each / per person / per trip / per night /
            per hour / per piece. Child price when per person.
            (prototype)

[SRV-P1-06] Two durations that disagree
  WHERE     Add Service with a resource
  HAPPENS   "Duration shown" is free text ("1 hour") and
            "Book the resource for (minutes)" says "Empty means 80".
            Catalog shows durations as "20", "2" with no unit.
  EXPECTED  One duration in minutes (picker: 30 min, 1 h, 2 h...).
            Resource is booked for that time. Show "1 h" in catalog.

[SRV-P1-07] No "New requests" column for guest requests
  WHERE     Today board
  HAPPENS   Columns: Scheduled, Assigned, Active, Done.
  EXPECTED  Prototype: New requests (from guests - accept / decline),
            To assign, Assigned, In progress, Done.
  NOTE      Please confirm if Guest App / website can request a
            service today. If yes, this column is needed.
