 WORKS - KEEP
================================================================
  - Money maths inside a booking: totals, advance 30%/50%, discount,
    change, balance, Partly paid / Paid labels.
  - Cashbox posts every cash payment with booking number - exact.
  - Collect payment: cash with change, bKash with reference.
  - Extend stay: financial impact, reason, confirm tick.
  - Add charge: preview of new total and balance.
  - Check-out blocked while balance or pending order exists.
  - Checked-out rooms turn Dirty; early-checkout room released.
  - Early check-in blocked with clear time.
  - Capacity check ("max 3"); multi-room booking; overpay blocked.
  - Counters, tabs, search, filters, "Needs attention", "Checkout
    overdue" / "Needs review" tags.
  - Guest step: saved guest search, ID type + photos, booked by / who
    pays, other guests, preferences.
  - Footer note "Booking status, payment and room cleaning are kept
    separate".
