---------------- P1 ----------------

[INV-P1-01] Receiving at a new price re-prices ALL old stock
  WHERE     Receive / PO receive
  HAPPENS   Item cost is replaced by the last price, for all stores.
            Example:
              Main Store had 50 kg rice @ ৳80 = ৳4,000
              Received 50 kg @ ৳90 into QA Kitchen
              -> Main Store's old 50 kg became ৳90 too (৳4,400 -> ৳4,900)
              Then PO receipt 25 kg @ ৳95 -> all 115 kg now @ ৳95
            Form text even says: "A unit cost entered here replaces
            the item's current unit cost."
  IMPACT    Stock value, food cost and cost of goods are wrong
            whenever prices change. Accounts get wrong numbers.
  EXPECTED  Weighted average cost (prototype):
            (old qty x old cost + new qty x new cost) / total qty
            Example above: (50x80 + 50x90) / 100 = ৳85
  FIX       BE: weighted average on every receipt. Movement keeps
            its own unit cost for history.

[INV-P1-02] Issue / Wastage / Adjust forms can change cost and supplier
  WHERE     Issue, Wastage, Physical count adjustment drawers
  HAPPENS   These forms show Supplier + Unit cost fields with
            "replaces the item's current unit cost".
            Supplier is saved on the movement: History shows
            "QA Fresh Supply" on Issued and Wastage lines.
  EXPECTED  Supplier + cost only on Receive. Issue/wastage use the
            current (average) cost automatically.
  FIX       FE: hide fields. BE: ignore cost/supplier for out moves.

[INV-P1-03] Wastage saved without a reason
  WHERE     Wastage
  HAPPENS   Saved with empty note. History shows "-".
  EXPECTED  Reason required for Wastage and Adjustment (prototype).
            Quick picks Damaged / Expired / Spilled already exist.
  FIX       FE + BE validation.

[INV-P1-04] Drawer title and close (X) hidden under the top bar
  WHERE     Stock count drawer, New Purchase Order drawer,
            Purchase order details, Add Recipe drawer
  HAPPENS   Drawer starts at top 0; the app header (63 px) covers
            its title (e.g. "CNT-0001 - QA Kitchen") and X button.
  EXPECTED  Same as Add Item / Transfer drawers: title + X visible.
  FIX       FE: z-index / top offset of these drawers.

[INV-P1-05] Buying unit (sack, carton...) not used anywhere
  WHERE     Purchase order, Receive
  HAPPENS   Item says "1 sack = 25 kg. Orders round up to whole
            sacks." But PO accepted 30 kg (= "1.2 sack").
            PO and Receive only allow kg, never sacks.
  EXPECTED  PO and Receive can enter in sacks; PO rounds up to
            whole buying units; stock is stored in kg.

[INV-P1-06] Opening stock always goes to Main Store
  WHERE     Add Item
  HAPPENS   No store choice for opening stock.
  EXPECTED  "Opening stock is in: [store]" (prototype) when
            opening stock > 0.

[INV-P1-07] Recipes are not linked to Restaurant & Bar (Meals)
  WHERE     Recipes & sales
  HAPPENS   Dish name is free text. Sales are typed by hand.
  EXPECTED  Recipe links to a Meals menu item. Every closed
            restaurant order takes ingredients out of stock
            automatically (prototype note). Manual sales stay as
            a backup.
  FIX       BE: integration Meals order -> recipe -> stock out.
