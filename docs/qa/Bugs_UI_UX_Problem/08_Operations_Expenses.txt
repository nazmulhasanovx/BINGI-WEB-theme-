  status change itself moves cash in/out of the Cashbox. Payments,
  approval and cash are not one locked flow.

 B. P0 - FIX FIRST
================================================================

[EXP-P0-01] Approval can be skipped by paying through the API
  WHERE    POST /api/operations/expenses/{id}/payments
  HAPPENS  EXP-000006 (12,000, above the 5,000 rule, "Waiting
           approval"). The UI correctly hides "Pay". A direct API
           call with amount 500 returned 201 "Part payment
           recorded" and the expense became status "approved",
           needs_approval false, approved_at set - nobody approved.
  IMPACT   Approval rule is only a UI rule. Any staff token can pay
           out and self-approve.
  EXPECTED 403/422 "Waiting for manager approval" until a manager
           approves. A payment must never change approval.
  FIX      BE: check needs_approval + approver role in the payment
           endpoint (and in supplier-bill pay). Never auto-approve.

[EXP-P0-02] Cash payment not posted to Cashbox
  WHERE    Same payment (EXP-000006, 500 Cash).
  HAPPENS  Payment saved, paid_amount 500, but Cashbox balance did
           not change and there is no movement for EXP-000006.
           The 1,200 cash payment on EXP-000005 was posted.
  IMPACT   Drawer is short 500 vs the system; day close will show
           a discrepancy nobody can explain.
  EXPECTED Every Cash payment = one Cashbox "cash out" movement,
           same transaction.
  FIX      BE: create the cash movement inside the payment service
           (not in the status/approval path); DB transaction so both
           save or both fail.

[EXP-P0-03] "Change status -> Paid" pays an unapproved expense in full
  WHERE    Expense panel > Change status dropdown.
  HAPPENS  EXP-000007 (6,000, needs approval). Choosing "Paid" sent
           PATCH status=completed, paid_amount=6000. Cash -6,000,
           no approval, no payment record (panel says "Paid before
           payment history (Cash)").
  IMPACT   Approval and payment history are both bypassed from the
           normal screen.
  EXPECTED Prototype: no status dropdown. Buttons only, in order:
           Approve / Reject (manager) -> Pay (creates payment).
           "Paid" is a result, not a choice.
  FIX      FE: remove the status dropdown. BE: block PATCH to
           paid/completed; status changes only via approve, reject,
           pay endpoints.

[EXP-P0-04] Rejecting or "Draft"-ing a PAID expense puts the cash back
  WHERE    Change status -> Draft / Rejected on a paid expense.
  HAPPENS  EXP-000007 paid 6,000. -> Draft: Cashbox +6,000 (expense
           still says paid 6,000). -> Paid again: -6,000. ->
           Rejected (reason optional, left empty): +6,000 again.
           Cashbox shows these as "cash in from QA Utilities".
  IMPACT   Easy way to hide cash taken from the drawer: pay, then
           reject. Cashbox looks balanced, drawer is short.
  EXPECTED A paid expense cannot be rejected or drafted. To undo,
           use "Record refund from payee" (cash in with reason,
           manager only), shown as its own line.
  FIX      BE: reject/draft only when paid_amount = 0. Refund =
           separate endpoint + Cashbox movement "Expense refund".

[EXP-P0-05] "Net Profit" is not profit
  WHERE    Overview card "Net Profit BDT 14,23,057.14"; "Revenue vs
           expense" chart (24 Nov revenue 13,49,912).
  HAPPENS  Revenue = total value of bookings CREATED in the period
           (incl. unpaid and future December stays; 30-day created,
           not cancelled = 14,45,835). Money actually received in
           the same period = about 5,30,380. Expenses side includes
           a REJECTED expense (6,000) and unpaid ones.
  IMPACT   Owner sees ~14 lakh "profit" with ~5 lakh collected.
           Wrong decisions.
  EXPECTED Prototype: "Left after costs = Received − spent −
           payroll" with the formula shown under the number.
  FIX      BE: received = payments in period (Payments module);
           spent = paid expenses; exclude rejected. Rename card to
           "Left after costs". Show the formula line.

[EXP-P1-01] Every new expense saved as "Waiting approval"
  WHERE    New expense > Save.
  HAPPENS  FE always sends status "pending". EXP-000005 (3,200,
           under the 5,000 rule) shows "Waiting approval" in list
           and panel, while API says needs_approval false.
           "Pending approvals 1" on Overview = "QA zero" (BDT 0).
  EXPECTED Under the limit -> "To pay" (or "Paid" if paid now).
           Only above the limit -> "Waiting approval".
  FIX      FE: don't send status; BE decides from the rule.

[EXP-P1-02] Rejected expense counted in totals
  WHERE    Overview: Total Expenses 24,375 and Paid 9,500 include
           EXP-000007 (rejected, 6,000). "Where the money went:
           QA Utilities 10,000" also includes it.
  FIX      BE: exclude rejected from totals and charts.

[EXP-P1-03] Panel shows stale numbers after a payment
  WHERE    Expense panel after "Record payment".
  HAPPENS  After paying 1,200 the panel still said Paid 0.00, Due
           3,200, "No payments yet", status "Waiting approval" until
           closed and reopened.
  FIX      FE: refresh the expense after payment (use response).

[EXP-P1-04] "bKash" listed twice in every payment-method list
  WHERE    New expense, Record payment, Pay supplier bill.
  FIX      BE/FE: de-duplicate payment methods (two bKash rows in
           settings?). Same list as Payments module.

[EXP-P1-05] Weak amount/date validation
  HAPPENS  Amount 0 accepted (EXP-000009). Date 1 Jun 2027 accepted
           (EXP-000010) and then hidden from all "30 days" views.
  FIX      BE: amount > 0; date not later than today + 1 (or warn).

[EXP-P1-06] Payroll is missing from Expenses
  WHERE    Overview.
  EXPECTED Prototype + plan D2: "Payroll · <month>" card (Net /
           Paid / Due, link "Open payroll"), and payroll counted in
           "Left after costs".
  FIX      BE: payroll cost from Staff; FE card.

[EXP-P1-07] No default categories
  HAPPENS  New hotel has 0 categories; user must create them before
           the first expense. Duplicate names allowed ("QA Utilities"
           and "qa utilities"). Supplier-bill payment silently
           creates/uses category "Supplies" which is not in the list.
  EXPECTED Prototype seeds 11: Utilities, Repairs & maintenance,
           Supplies, Food & beverage purchase, Laundry, Transport,
           Staff food & welfare, Marketing, Rent, Bank & fees, Other.
  FIX      BE: seed defaults per property; unique name
           (case-insensitive).

[EXP-P1-08] Payment method asked at creation means nothing
  WHERE    New expense > Payment method (Cash).
  HAPPENS  Choosing Cash does not pay anything; Paid stays 0. Users
           will think the expense is paid.
  EXPECTED Prototype form has no method. Either remove it, or add
           "Paid now? [Yes / No]" - Yes creates the payment (and the
           Cashbox movement) at once.

[EXP-P1-09] Page name "Operations" (decision D4 = "Expenses")
  WHERE    Sidebar, page title, breadcrumb.
  EXPECTED Prototype + HMS_Remaining_Modules_Plan D4: label
           "Expenses", order unchanged.
