  WHERE     Housekeeping > Maintenance  vs  Rooms > Room numbers
  HAPPENS   - Room 202 = "Not for sale / Maintenance" in Rooms (legacy
              HOLD). Housekeeping: "Room blocks 0", no ticket for 202.
              Room panel says "Review it in Housekeeping" -> nothing
              there. No Release button anywhere.
            - Room 207 has OPEN URGENT ticket mnt_dsz2mpsrwn ("ggg",
              target 9 Nov - overdue) with Room block Off -> 207 is
              "Available" and has a confirmed booking WBK-000080
              (26-28 Nov).
  IMPACT    One room lost from sale for ever; another sold with an
            urgent open fault.
  EXPECTED  An OOO block always has a maintenance record (reason +
            expected back) and a "Release" action. An urgent open
            ticket shows a warning on the room and in New booking.
  FIX       BE: migrate legacy HOLD into maintenance blocks (or clear);
            availability reads the maintenance block.
            FE: warning chip "Open fault" on room cards.
  (same item as ROOM-P0-05)
